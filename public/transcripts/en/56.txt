（What Happened on a Trip）
The other day, I traveled alone for the first time. I wanted to see snow, so I did a lot of research online and decided to go to Sapporo. The Sapporo Snow Festival was going on, and I thought it sounded fun. There are hot springs nearby too. What surprised me when I got there was, of course, the cold. It was so cold that it was nothing like the cold around here. I wanted to see the snow sculptures lit up, so I went out at night, but it was so cold that after walking for less than ten minutes, my body started shaking. I was wearing lots of layers and a down coat, but halfway through I couldn't stand it anymore and rushed into a tent with a heater. But I was really, really surprised to see a girl who looked like a local high school student walking with bare legs in a short skirt. The sight of that high school girl might have been the most surprising thing in Sapporo.
（Impressions of a Movie）
The other day I saw a movie called "Shout Out Loud," and it was pretty good. The main character is a soccer player, so in terms of genre, it would be a sports movie. It's about a really weak soccer team with no money, and nobody has any confidence, so they give up on games before they even start. Among them there's just one player with a hungry spirit, and the team gradually changes, but it doesn't have a so-called happy ending. Yeah, it's not just "they worked hard and succeeded"; it also shows the players' dark, messy side. I felt it was really realistic. I won't say any more because it'd be a spoiler, but I think it's worth watching. Really, I recommend it. It'll change your outlook on life.
