（Stating an Opinion）
Lately I often hear the opinion that it is not good to let elementary school students have smartphones. Is that really true? I think we should also consider the good side of smartphones. For example, if children have a smartphone, they can contact their parents right away in a dangerous situation. It also becomes easy for parents to know where their child is. Furthermore, by using smartphones from a young age, children can develop IT literacy. Admittedly, smartphones also have risks. Children may play games too much or connect with dangerous people on social media. However, couldn't these problems be sufficiently prevented if parents pay attention? Thinking about it this way, I believe that letting elementary school students have smartphones does more good than harm.
（At an Interview: Motivation for Studying Japanese）
What got me interested in Japan was the Japanese anime I watched as a child. The one I still remember best is an anime called "Flower." I thought all Japanese people wore such cute clothes and had such cute hairstyles. Looking back, it was a childish idea, but to me at the time, it was a life I longed for. After that, I started working in my home country, but after a while I began to feel that my work was unfulfilling. That was when I found an advertisement recruiting students for a Japanese language school, and my passion for Japan was rekindled. Of course, I know that the Japan in anime is not reality. But through studying Japanese, I want to become a new version of myself and, in the future, find rewarding work that suits me. If possible, I would like to do work that helps protect the environment. That is my motivation for studying Japanese.
